AIML Practical Relevance

In machine learning, the Binomial distribution governs Bernoulli trials. It is foundational in evaluating classifier accuracy over test samples, calculating confidence intervals for model evaluation metrics, and building Naive Bayes feature likelihoods for binary text classification.
